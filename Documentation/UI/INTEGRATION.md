## Real existing-game connections

- Main profile and wallet read the local model; combat kills earn gold and update live quests.
- The five upgrade stats are attack, health, health regeneration, 2x critical chance and 4x critical chance. Attack-speed and defense stat rows have been removed. The attack-speed multiplier stays at 1, preserving the existing game's attack interval. Old collection saves migrate defense levels to health regeneration and speed levels to 2x critical chance; recognized ownership and upgrade data remain saved.
- Attack upgrades and applicable equipment, skill, companion, relic and skin bonuses affect the existing combat damage modifier. Fresh starter damage remains normalized to the original arena baseline. Each actual damage event checks 4x critical first, then checks 2x only if 4x did not occur; the two results never multiply together. Critical-damage bonuses apply only to critical hits. Both probabilities are capped at 100%, and displayed power uses the same priority-weighted expected multiplier.
- Eight displayed equipped skills use the corresponding real combat cooldown clocks. Equipping changes the loadout bonus and HUD. The original automatic23 skills remain active to preserve existing combat; this is explicitly explained in skill detail.
- The three dungeons are Gold Cave, Diamond Cave and Relic Cave, identified by yellow, blue and purple keys with matching text labels. Each has three independent daily attempts. Entry starts a real existing-field kill challenge: the current tuning requires 30, 40 and 50 kills respectively. Clears award 30a gold, 500 diamonds or one relic summon ticket. Relic tickets have their own saved wallet and can be spent on the shop's one-ticket relic draw; they do not spend diamonds. No new independent dungeon maps are claimed.
- Main stages count completed 30-kill field waves, starting at zero completed stages. Surplus kills carry into the next main wave. During an active dungeon, kills advance that dungeon only, and each clear increases its own saved completed-stage count by one. HighestDungeonStage is the maximum of the three dungeon counts, not their sum. Daily attempt resets do not reset stage progress.
- The main HUD starts with the attack-level-15 mission. Claiming a completed mission awards exactly 500 diamonds, saves its completion index, and advances to the next objective. Subsequent cycles use increasing field-kill, earned-gold, attack-level and main-stage goals. The next goal can already be complete through accumulated progress, but each mission is paid once. Daily, repeat and weekly quest rewards are also 500 diamonds per completed quest. Repeat quests display pending completed cycles separately from the remainder: 1501 kills toward a 500-kill goal means three pending rewards and 1/500 progress. Individual and bulk claims pay all completed cycles that fit the wallet, preserve unpaid cycles and remainder, and retain the existing saved counter format.
- Relic enhancement consumes one copy of that same relic per attempt, with a fixed 50% success chance and no gold charge. Success adds one level; failure consumes the copy but keeps the existing level. Discovered relic effects continue to apply without an equipment slot, and the relic panel displays their totals.
- Weapon and appearance skins have separate ownership and equipped selections. All owned skin bonuses apply whether equipped or not; equipping changes the world weapon sprite/tint or the animated cat's appearance tint, without adding the ownership bonus twice. Diamond skins charge once on acquisition. Main-stage skins require 100 completed main stages; dungeon-stage skins require one dungeon to reach 100 completed stages. Meeting a requirement enables the explicit unlock action, and ownership/equipped choices persist.
- Active buffs expire by UTC time; gold and damage bonuses apply to real combat/gains. An active buff displays a disabled '활성화 중' button and cannot be extended or charged again, including through direct API calls. When it expires, the open popup updates back to the activation button. Existing saved expiry timestamps remain compatible.
- The saved three-state camera control changes the real orthographic camera size to 8.5, 11 or 14.5. Upgrade/acquisition changes show the before/after power and its difference.
- UI button/dim gestures block pointer movement, release the joystick, suppress gameplay keyboard shortcuts while modal UI is open, and consume the dismissing release.
- Stat quantity choices are x1, x10, x100 and MAX; MAX computes the affordable remaining upgrades from the actual escalating prices. Bound upgrade buttons support hold-to-repeat after 0.4 seconds, then one transaction every 0.12 seconds at most. Releasing, leaving the button, dragging, changing pages or running out of resources stops repetition; the release cannot add an extra purchase. DOTween drives button press/rebound and popup open/close motion using unscaled time. Closing panels immediately stop accepting input while their short visual exit finishes.

## Explicit external and baseline limitations

- Currency products display price/quantity, but payment provider is unconnected. The purchase button explains that no charge or grant occurs.
- Account linking is unconnected and labelled accordingly.
- Chat is a local, session-only input demonstration and sends nothing to a network.
- PVP ranks and opponents are a labelled local simulation; podium art is taken from the same opponent records shown in the ranking list. No online leaderboard or real matchmaking is claimed.
- The existing game has no player health/damage receiver. Health and health-regeneration stats and their relic/skin bonuses currently affect the model and displayed power; they do not introduce actual player damage, healing or death. Adding a player survival system would change combat scope.
- The existing game provides no background music/SFX AudioSources. Volume sliders save/apply to available sources; the missing audio is stated on screen. Power saving switches the real frame-rate target between 30 and 60 FPS. Settings no longer contains pause/resume buttons; its quit confirmation saves progress before quitting the application.
- Device-local date/saves are not authoritative server time or anti-cheat protected economy.
