## Dirt floor

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless square ground texture for a top-down 2D doodle idle game. Generate a 1024x1024 tileable image of flat pale warm beige dirt ground, viewed straight down. Entire image is ONLY ground, edge to edge, NO horizon, NO walls, NO characters, NO UI, NO text, NO large objects, NO borders. Match the provided reference's naive slightly wobbly hand-drawn black pen cartoon style, but the ground has subtle low-contrast warm brown thin sketch marks so white outlined characters read easily on it. Very sparse tiny pebble marks, a few little uneven earth patches and short 2-stroke sparse dry grass doodles. 90 percent calm empty pale sand beige floor. Matte flat color, barely perceptible paper grain, no realistic perspective, no photorealism, no strong shading or shadows, no dense noise. All four edges should match for seamless tiling.
